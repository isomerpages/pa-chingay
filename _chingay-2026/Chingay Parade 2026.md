<p>This year, witness the communities unite through the power of our wishes.
Be greeted by awe-inspiring Parade of multicultural heritage with modern
creativity through <strong>illuminated costumes and props</strong>, <strong>dynamic artistic performances</strong>,
and <strong>show-stopping floats</strong>. Experience Chingay's <strong>first-ever circular parade route</strong>,
featuring special effects that light up the night and the inaugural <strong>ASEAN Combined Percussion Ensemble</strong> performing
on a <strong>transformable multi-tier stage</strong>. More than just a parade,
Chingay 2026 is our nation’s grandest cultural celebration – an explosion
of joy and creativity you won’t want to miss.</p>
